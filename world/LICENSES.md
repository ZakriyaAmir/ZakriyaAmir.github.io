## Character
The ninja-kid character (`ninja.js`) is original and built procedurally in code: skinned meshes (torso, arms, legs) plus rigid accessories on a THREE.Bone rig, with procedurally authored animation clips. It is a stylistic homage to "Ninja Kid" archetypes. It uses no Halfbrick / Fruit Ninja assets, models, textures or sounds.
